How it's calculated

Total tip pool = legal tips (10% of sales) + additional tips
Tip rate = total tip pool ÷ total hours worked by everyone in the pool
Each payout = that person's hours × tip rate
  • Payouts always add up to the whole pool; nothing is held back.
  • Figures stay on this device until you start a new period. Nothing is sent anywhere.
